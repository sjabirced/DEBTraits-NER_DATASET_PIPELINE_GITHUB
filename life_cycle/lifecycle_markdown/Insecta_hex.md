# Insecta — DEB Model: HEX

**Class:** Insecta  
**DEB model:** `hex` — DEB model for holometabolic insects — complete metamorphosis; larva allocates to the reproduction buffer; pupal stage.

## Life Cycle Timeline

1. **t0** (transition) — *Fertilisation*
   Origin of the life cycle. The egg is fertilised and embryonic development begins; all energy is supplied by internal yolk or maternal reserves.

2. **Egg / Incubation** (stage — DEB: *Embryo*)
   The embryo develops inside the egg, fully dependent on yolk for energy. In many species this phase involves external incubation by a parent or the environment.

3. **B** (transition) — *Hatching into larva*
   The larva hatches from the egg and enters an accelerating larval phase in which it allocates energy to the reproduction buffer rather than to maturation.

4. **Larval stage** (stage — DEB: *Larva (Acceleration)*)
   The organism grows under V1-morphic metabolic acceleration, with exceptionally rapid growth driven by accelerated surface-area-to-volume dynamics.

5. **J** (transition) — *Metamorphosis*
   Metamorphosis is complete. Metabolic acceleration ends and the organism adopts its post-larval form; standard isomorphic growth resumes towards puberty.

6. **Post-metamorphosis juv.** (stage — DEB: *Juvenile*)
   Post-metamorphosis isomorphic juvenile phase. Standard growth resumes after larval acceleration ends and the organism matures towards puberty.

7. **P** (transition) — *Pupation triggered*
   The larval reproduction buffer reaches a critical density, triggering the onset of the pupal stage; no further larval growth occurs.

8. **Pupal stage** (stage — DEB: *Pupa*)
   Complete internal reorganisation inside the puparium or chrysalis. The organism does not feed and does not grow while larval tissues are dismantled and rebuilt into adult structures.

9. **U** (transition) — *Pupal eclosion*
   The pupa completes its internal reorganisation. Larval tissues have been dismantled and rebuilt into adult structures; the imago is ready to emerge.

10. **Emergence** (stage — DEB: *Eclosion*)
   The imago physically breaks free of the pupal case, marking the completion of holometabolic metamorphosis.

11. **E** (transition) — *Imago emergence*
   The adult form (imago) emerges from the pupal case. It will not grow and does not reproduce; it uses accumulated reserves for maintenance, dispersal and mating.

12. **Winged adult (imago)** (stage — DEB: *Imago*)
   The fully formed adult (imago) uses its energy reserves for maintenance, dispersal and reproductive activities. No somatic growth occurs.

13. **M** (transition) — *Natural death*
   Natural death. The organism reaches the end of its reproductive life; senescence or post-reproductive mortality marks the conclusion of the DEB life cycle.
