# Malacostraca — DEB Model: STD

**Class:** Malacostraca  
**DEB model:** `std` — Standard DEB model — isomorphic throughout; three phases: embryo, juvenile, adult.

## Life Cycle Timeline

1. **t0** (transition) — *Fertilisation*
   Origin of the life cycle. The egg is fertilised and embryonic development begins; all energy is supplied by internal yolk or maternal reserves.

2. **Egg** (stage — DEB: *Embryo*)
   Protected by the egg shell, the embryo is entirely enclosed and sustained by internal yolk reserves. No external feeding occurs.

3. **H** (transition) — *Hatching*
   The organism breaks free from the egg but has not yet started external feeding. It continues to draw on internal yolk reserves and remains fully yolk-dependent.

4. **Pre-feeding larva** (stage — DEB: *Embryo*)
   Although hatched, the organism has not yet begun external feeding and continues depleting its internal yolk reserves before independent life begins.

5. **B** (transition) — *Hatching / First feeding*
   The organism begins feeding from the external environment for the first time; independent life truly starts and somatic growth accelerates under available food.

6. **Juvenile** (stage — DEB: *Juvenile*)
   Standard isomorphic growth phase. Energy is directed towards body size increase and maturation towards puberty.

7. **P** (transition) — *Sexual maturity*
   First reproduction event. Energy allocation shifts permanently from somatic growth to reproduction; the organism is now sexually mature.

8. **Breeding adult** (stage — DEB: *Adult*)
   Full reproductive adult phase. Somatic growth has ceased and energy allocation is directed to reproduction, maintenance and survival.

9. **M** (transition) — *Natural death*
   Natural death. The organism reaches the end of its reproductive life; senescence or post-reproductive mortality marks the conclusion of the DEB life cycle.
