# Mammalia — DEB Model: STX

**Class:** Mammalia  
**DEB model:** `stx` — stf with baby stage and weaning — adds a post-birth lactation phase ending at weaning.

## Life Cycle Timeline

1. **t0** (transition) — *Fertilisation*
   Origin of the life cycle. The egg is fertilised and embryonic development begins; all energy is supplied by internal yolk or maternal reserves.

2. **Gestation** (stage — DEB: *Embryo*)
   Embryonic development occurs inside the mother during a bounded gestation period; the mother transfers resources to the embryo until live birth.

3. **G** (transition) — *Live birth (parturition)*
   End of the gestation period. The offspring exits the uterus after a measurable gestation duration; the mother's direct embryonic investment is complete.

4. **Neonatal** (stage — DEB: *Neonate*)
   A very brief post-birth window before nursing begins. The newborn is entirely dependent on the mother and not yet feeding independently.

5. **B** (transition) — *Nursing onset*
   Following live birth, the neonate begins nursing. The lactation phase starts and the offspring depends on maternal milk as its sole energy source.

6. **Suckling / Nursing** (stage — DEB: *Juvenile (Nursing)*)
   The neonate feeds exclusively on maternal milk. Growth is rapid and the organism is entirely dependent on the mother for energy.

7. **X** (transition) — *Weaning*
   Weaning. Milk dependence ends and the juvenile transitions permanently to independent solid-food feeding.

8. **Juvenile** (stage — DEB: *Juvenile*)
   Standard isomorphic growth phase. Energy is directed towards body size increase and maturation towards puberty.

9. **P** (transition) — *Sexual maturity*
   First reproduction event. Energy allocation shifts permanently from somatic growth to reproduction; the organism is now sexually mature.

10. **Breeding adult** (stage — DEB: *Adult*)
   Full reproductive adult phase. Somatic growth has ceased and energy allocation is directed to reproduction, maintenance and survival.

11. **M** (transition) — *Natural death*
   Natural death. The organism reaches the end of its reproductive life; senescence or post-reproductive mortality marks the conclusion of the DEB life cycle.
