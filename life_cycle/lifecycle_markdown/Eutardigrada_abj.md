**Class:** Eutardigrada  
**DEB model:** `abj` — std with type M metabolic acceleration — V1-morphic acceleration between birth and metamorphosis.

## Life Cycle Timeline

1. **t0** (transition) — *Fertilisation*
   Origin of the life cycle. The egg is fertilised and embryonic development begins; all energy is supplied by internal yolk or maternal reserves.

2. **Egg / Incubation** (stage — DEB: *Embryo*)
   The embryo develops inside the egg, fully dependent on yolk for energy. In many species this phase involves external incubation by a parent or the environment.

3. **B** (transition) — *Hatching / First feeding*
   The organism begins feeding from the external environment for the first time; independent life truly starts and somatic growth accelerates under available food.

4. **Larval stage** (stage — DEB: *Larva (Acceleration)*)
   The organism grows under V1-morphic metabolic acceleration, with exceptionally rapid growth driven by accelerated surface-area-to-volume dynamics.

5. **J** (transition) — *Metamorphosis*
   Metamorphosis is complete. Metabolic acceleration ends and the organism adopts its post-larval form; standard isomorphic growth resumes towards puberty.

6. **Post-metamorphosis juv.** (stage — DEB: *Juvenile*)
   Post-metamorphosis isomorphic juvenile phase. Standard growth resumes after larval acceleration ends and the organism matures towards puberty.

7. **P** (transition) — *Sexual maturity*
   First reproduction event. Energy allocation shifts permanently from somatic growth to reproduction; the organism is now sexually mature.

8. **Breeding adult** (stage — DEB: *Adult*)
   Full reproductive adult phase. Somatic growth has ceased and energy allocation is directed to reproduction, maintenance and survival.

9. **M** (transition) — *Natural death*
   Natural death. The organism reaches the end of its reproductive life; senescence or post-reproductive mortality marks the conclusion of the DEB life cycle.
